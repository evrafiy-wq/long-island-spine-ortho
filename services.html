Reference Guide

Treated Conditions

Explore common musculoskeletal conditions and typical treatment approaches provided at our practice.

Herniated Disc

Soft center of a spinal disc pushes through a tear in the outer ring, compressing nerves.

  • Symptoms: Sciatica, leg pain, numbness
  • Approaches: PT, injections, microdiscectomy

Spinal Stenosis

Narrowing of spinal spaces restricting the spinal cord and nerve roots.

  • Symptoms: Cramping when walking, stiffness
  • Approaches: Injections, surgical decompression

Degenerative Disc Disease

Gradual wearing down of spinal discs, resulting in loss of shock absorption.

  • Symptoms: Ache worse with sitting or bending
  • Approaches: Facet blocks, stabilization therapy

Knee Osteoarthritis

Cartilage breakdown in the knee joint leads to pain, stiffness, and reduced mobility.

  • Symptoms: Joint pain, swelling, stiffness after rest
  • Approaches: PT, injections, activity modification

Rotator Cuff Injury

Strain or tearing of the shoulder tendons that stabilize the joint.

  • Symptoms: Shoulder weakness, pain with overhead motion
  • Approaches: PT, bracing, surgical repair

Hip Impingement

Abnormal contact between the hip ball and socket limits comfortable range of motion.

  • Symptoms: Groin pain, stiffness with hip flexion
  • Approaches: PT, activity modification, injections

Physical Therapy & Bracing

Guided exercise and supportive bracing to restore strength and stability.

  • Best for: Early-stage joint and spine conditions

Joint & Epidural Injections

Targeted corticosteroid or anti-inflammatory injections to reduce pain and swelling.

  • Best for: Localized inflammation or nerve irritation

Activity Modification

A tailored home exercise and lifestyle plan to manage symptoms conservatively.

  • Best for: Mild or chronic musculoskeletal concerns
