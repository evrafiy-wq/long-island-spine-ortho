Reference Guide

Treated Conditions

Explore common musculoskeletal conditions and typical treatment approaches provided at our facility.

Herniated Disc

Soft center of a spinal disc pushes through a tear in the outer ring, compressing nerves.

Symptoms

Sciatica, leg pain, numbness

Approaches

PT, injections, Microdiscectomy

Spinal Stenosis

Narrowing of spinal spaces restricting the spinal cord and nerve roots.

Symptoms

Cramping when walking, stiffness

Approaches

Injections, surgical decompression

Disc Disease

Gradual wearing down of spinal discs, resulting in loss of shock-absorption.

Symptoms

Ache worse with sitting or bending

Approaches

Facet blocks, stabilization therapy
